# FAQ

**What is CRM Builder?**
Desktop app to design custom CRM systems without coding.

**Is it free?**
Yes for up to 3 users. From EUR 10/mo for 4+.

**Platforms?**
Windows, macOS, Linux.

**Where is data stored?**
On your machine. Self-hosted SQLite. No cloud.

**Can I import data?**
Yes. CSV, Excel, SQLite.

**Mobile version?**
Planned.

**How are users counted?**
Each active account.

**Refund?**
14 days.
