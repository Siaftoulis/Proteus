## Notes / Log

### 2026-07-04: Spin-Up Analysis

**Issues Found:**

1. **Phase 1 has ZERO user-visible output.** If we wait until Phase 1 is complete (2-3 months), there will be nothing to show. No screenshots, no demo, no progress. Marketing cannot build hype around invisible infrastructure.

2. **No brand identity.** No name (besides "CRM Builder"), no logo, no color palette, no tagline. Can't create a landing page without brand.

3. **No competitor analysis.** Who is the competition? What do they charge? What do they miss? This is essential for positioning but doesn't exist.

4. **No unique value proposition.** "Visual CRM Builder" is generic. Why would someone choose this over Airtable? Over Monday? Over Notion? No answer.

5. **Product Hunt requires a demo.** A PH launch needs a working product, screenshots, and preferably a video. If we launch after Phase 1 (which has no UI changes), the demo will look identical to the alpha. No "wow" factor.

6. **Lifetime discount of EUR 99 is not a launch strategy.** That's a tactic. A strategy includes: who to target, where to find them, what message to use.

**Recommendations:**
- Document a brand identity (name + logo + tagline) before Phase 1 ends. Create it during Phase 1, not after.
- Write a comparison table vs. Airtable/Monday/Notion now
- Plan a "preview" campaign during Phase 1: dev blog posts about the foundation work, architecture stories
- Define a specific niche for the Product Hunt launch (e.g., "for small teams that need custom CRMs")
- Target EU market first (timezone, language, payment)

- 2026-07-04: Marketing plan drafted. Not started.
- 2026-07-04: Budget: EUR 0. Organic growth only.
- 2026-07-04: CEO: no marketing until Phase 1 done.
